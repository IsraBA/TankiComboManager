Significantly boosts the effects of all types of supplies.

This drone consumes additional units of supplies upon activation. Your tank can choose one of three operating modes. It can deal more damage, be faster, or get less damage, depending on which mode you choose. When you switch to one of the supplies, all others get deactivated. Upgrading this drone boosts the effect of «Boosted Damage» and «Boosted Armor» supplies.

It requires a lot of skill, giving you unmatched tactical flexibility in return.
